# Time-Capsule Radio: "Echoes of the Web Past"

## The Concept: 
An ambient newsfeed and podcast generator that fetches front-page snapshots from news outlets and blogs on today's date from 10, 20, or 30 years ago via the Wayback Machine. An open LLM synthesizes a "spooky historical dispatch" contrasting past predictions with today's reality, broadcast in a radio style.


## Tech Stack: 

ElevenLabs (vintage/ghostly voice synthesis) + Gemma (trend analysis & scriptwriting) + DigitalOcean or MongoDB Atlas (storing historical diffs).

## Open Innovation Pitch: 
Paywalls and shifting media ownership threaten digital news history. Open-weight tools combined with public web archives make offline historical analysis accessible without subscription gatekeepers.
